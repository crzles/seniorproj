Problem/Opportunity Statement: This application will assist individuals who are on short-term and long-term medication/supplements. 
Helps track their dosage schedule and allow them to register symptoms that arise during treatment.  

Background: There are many individuals of different ages around us who struggle with health problems that may or may not be visible 
to everyone else. They may already feel overwhelmed every day with their daily duties of taking medications and tracking symptoms. 
Many keep a health diary to keep track of their symptoms or use alarms as reminders, but these tools can easily be forgotten or lost. 
This is our motive behind our project idea; we want to create a tool that will help ease the user, making it easy for them to navigate and use daily. 

Proposed Solution: Our team proposes to create a Supplement/Medication Tracker application that will help users keep track of their medications and supplements in one place. 
The application will allow users to add their medications or supplements, enter the dosage and schedule, and receive reminders when it is time to take them. Users will also 
be able to record symptoms they experience while taking their medications or supplements. This will give users an organized way to keep track of their treatment without having 
to rely on separate apps, alarms, or a physical health diary. 

Another feature of the application will be an interaction checker. Users will be able to select medications or supplements from their list, and the system will check them against 
our own interaction dataset. If a possible interaction is found, the application will notify the user. The application will also include a pharmacy locator using the Google Places API. 
This will allow users to find nearby pharmacies when they need to refill a medication. These features will be connected to the user's account so their information can be managed in one 
place. 

To determine if our application is working correctly, we will test each of the main features individually and together. We will test adding and editing medications, setting schedules, 
receiving reminders, recording symptoms, checking for interactions, and finding nearby pharmacies. We will also test incorrect or missing information to make sure the system responds 
properly. In addition to functional testing, we will have users test the application to see if the features are easy to understand and navigate. Our goal is for the main features to 
work correctly and for users to be able to complete common tasks without difficulty.
